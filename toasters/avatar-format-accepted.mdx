---
title: "Avatar photo formats accepted"
description: "Personal avatars are created from one photo, not a video"
---

Argil does not accept a training video for a new avatar. `.mov` and `.mp4` uploads are not the way to create one.

Use one photo:

- Formats: PNG, JPEG, or WEBP
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 9:16
- Resolution: 720p minimum, 1080p ideally, up to 4K
- One person, whole face in frame

See [Create an avatar from scratch](/resources/create-an-avatar).
