<Warning>
  Argil does not create avatars from a recorded training video. You do not capture 2 or 3 minutes of speech, and there is no video upload for avatar training. That flow is retired on every plan, including Pro.
</Warning>

A personal avatar is created from **one photo**. In the app, choose **Upload your photo**. The prompt is: "Upload just one photo of yourself to create your avatar."

You can also create an AI influencer from a text prompt instead of a photo. That path spends credits to generate the image. It is not a video upload.

<Tip>
  Photo checklist and both creation paths: [Create an avatar from scratch](/resources/create-an-avatar).
</Tip>

### Photo tips

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use one clear photo">
    Upload a single photo of one person. The whole face should be in the frame. Do not upload a video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Light the face">
    Face a window or another soft light source. Avoid heavy shadows across the face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay close">
    Closer photos produce a better avatar. Aim for 1080p. 720p is the minimum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the mouth natural">
    A neutral expression with the mouth slightly open, as if mid-sentence, works better than a wide smile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If creation looks stuck

Image avatars are usually ready within a minute. If one stays unfinished, contact [support@argil.ai](mailto:support@argil.ai). Do not re-record a training video. There is nowhere to upload it.
