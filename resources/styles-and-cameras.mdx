---
title: "Add styles and camera angles to your avatar"
description: "Styles are created from a photo or a prompt, not from a training video"
---

<Warning>
  Argil does not add styles or camera angles by recording a training video. You do not film a front view and a side angle and upload both.
</Warning>

Styles keep your face and place you in a different environment, outfit, or action. Create them from the avatar you already made with one photo, or from an AI influencer prompt.

See [Creating avatar styles](/resources/create-avatar-styles) and [Edit avatar styles](/resources/edit-avatar-styles).
