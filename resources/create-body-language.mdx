title: "Create body language on your avatar"
description: "Recorded gesture training is no longer available"
---

<Warning>
  Recording a training video to add gestures is no longer supported. Argil does not ask you to film a base video, extra gesture clips, or a combined training upload.
</Warning>

Create a personal avatar from one photo instead. See [Create an avatar from scratch](/resources/create-an-avatar).

Gesture labels taken from a dataset video belonged to the retired video avatar model. That control is not offered in the app today. Styles are added from a photo or a prompt, not from a recorded training take. See [Creating avatar styles](/resources/create-avatar-styles).
