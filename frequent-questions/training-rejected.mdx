---
title: "My training has been rejected and I don’t understand why"
description: "Video avatar training is no longer offered"
---

Argil does not accept a training video for a new avatar. A rejection email about face position, cuts, or sound quality refers to the retired video-to-video flow. You cannot fix that by re-recording.

Create a personal avatar from one photo instead. See [Create an avatar from scratch](/resources/create-an-avatar).

If a photo avatar fails, contact [support@argil.ai](mailto:support@argil.ai). Use a single clear photo: whole face in frame, good light, 720p or higher.
