Argil creates a personal avatar from one photo, not from a dataset video and a consent video.

Image avatars are usually ready within a minute. If one you created from a photo is still unfinished after that, contact [support@argil.ai](mailto:support@argil.ai).

There is no video upload to retry. Do not record a training video.
